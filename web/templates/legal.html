Warning: Trading financial instruments involves substantial risk of loss and is not suitable for all investors. 1. General Trading Risks

Trading in securities, including stocks, ETFs, and other financial instruments, involves substantial risk of loss. Key risks include:

  • You may lose some or all of the funds you deposit for trading
  • Past performance is not indicative of future results
  • No trading system or strategy can guarantee profits or prevent losses
  • Market conditions can change rapidly and without warning
  • Historical backtesting results may not reflect future live trading performance

2. AI and Algorithmic Trading Risks

The use of AI and algorithmic trading systems introduces specific risks:

  • AI models may produce inaccurate predictions or signals based on incomplete or biased data
  • Algorithms may behave unexpectedly in market conditions not represented in training data
  • Machine learning models may experience "model drift" as market dynamics evolve
  • Automated execution may result in rapid, compounding losses during adverse conditions
  • Technical failures in AI systems may lead to missed opportunities or erroneous trades

3. Market Volatility Risks

Financial markets are inherently volatile. You should be aware that:

  • Prices can fluctuate significantly in short periods due to economic events, geopolitical developments, or market sentiment
  • Volatility can result in execution at prices materially different from expected (slippage)
  • Market gaps (overnight or weekend) may result in orders being filled at prices substantially different from the prior close
  • Extreme market events ("flash crashes," circuit breakers) may prevent timely execution of orders

4. Liquidity Risks

Liquidity risk refers to the possibility that you may not be able to buy or sell a security at the desired price or in the desired quantity:

  • Low-volume securities may have wide bid-ask spreads, increasing transaction costs
  • Market conditions may reduce liquidity, making it difficult to exit positions
  • Small-cap and micro-cap securities may have limited market depth

5. Technology Risks

As a technology-dependent platform, Ferchaud is subject to the following risks:

  • System failures: Hardware, software, or network failures may disrupt the Service
  • Connectivity: Internet outages or latency may prevent order execution or data retrieval
  • Third-party dependencies: Outages at Alpaca, data providers, or cloud infrastructure may impact service availability
  • Cybersecurity: Despite security measures, no system is immune to cyberattacks or data breaches
  • Data accuracy: Market data feeds may contain errors, delays, or gaps that could affect trading decisions

6. Regulatory Risks

The regulatory landscape for trading platforms and AI-driven financial services is evolving:

  • Changes in securities regulations may affect the availability or operation of the Service
  • New laws or regulatory actions may impose additional requirements or restrictions
  • Tax laws and reporting requirements may change and affect your obligations

Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Ferchaud uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit or use our platform.

These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function and cannot be disabled:

  • Authentication token (token): A secure, HTTP-only cookie containing your JWT session token. Expires after 30 days. Required to maintain your logged-in state.
  • Session state: Temporary cookies used to maintain session context during your visit (e.g., CSRF protection, onboarding step tracking).

3. Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with the platform:

  • Page views and navigation patterns

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions

Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from logging in and using the Service.
