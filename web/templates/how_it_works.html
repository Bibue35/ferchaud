01 / Signal generation

13 strategies + a 12,000-symbol scanner

Every 10 seconds the bot pulls fresh OHLCV bars (1m, 5m, 15m, 1H) from Alpaca and runs each enabled strategy against every symbol it tracks. Strategies are pure functions of price & volume — no opinions, just rules.

The strategies

  • Mean-reversion · Bollinger pierce + RSI oversold/overbought, exits at the midband.
  • Momentum · EMA(9/21) crossover + MACD histogram + ADX > 20 + volume confirmation.
  • Statistical arbitrage · Cointegration tests + z-score mean-reversion on hardcoded pairs.
  • Multi-factor ML · LightGBM ranks the cross-section on 25 engineered features. Triple-barrier labels. Purged walk-forward CV.
  • Scalper · 5-min RSI extremes + VWAP reversion. Tight stops, quick targets.
  • Catalyst / Options catalyst · News + insider-buying signals. Optional options leg.
  • Aggressive breakout · 8-signal stack including X/Twitter sentiment from Grok.
  • Predictive short · 3-layer detection: research → confirm → enter at exhaustion.

The full-market scanner refreshes every 3 minutes and surfaces the top movers, volume spikes, and gap candidates — feeding strategies a fresh universe instead of trading the same 10 stocks all day.

Audit trail

Every signal is logged

The signal journal in your dashboard records every (strategy, symbol, signal-set, score, outcome) tuple. You can filter by strategy and rebuild any decision after the fact.

02 / AI ratification

Claude (or Grok) reads every candidate trade

Strategies don't trade directly. They produce candidates. Each candidate is sent to a frontier LLM with the full context: technicals, regime, recent winrate on this signal combo, the strategy's recent mistake distribution, portfolio exposure, and cash on hand. The LLM returns a structured JSON object.

// What the LLM gets { "candidate": { "strategy": "momentum", "symbol": "NVDA", "side": "long", "entry": 842.30, "atr": 6.2, "signals": { "ema_cross": 1, "macd_pos": 1, "adx_strong": 1 } }, "regime": "bull", "strategy_winrate_30d": 0.62, "strategy_recent_mistakes": { "sold_too_early": 4, "good": 18 }, "portfolio": { "equity": 12450, "cash": 4200, "open_positions": 3 } } // What the LLM returns { "action": "enter", "side": "long", "confidence": 0.78, "size_mult": 1.30, "rationale": "Strong momentum confluence in bull regime. 62% winrate over 22 trades. Recent sold-too-early bias suggests wider target.", "risks": ["NVDA earnings in 4 days", "high BB squeeze — vol breakout possible"] }

Provider fallback chain: Claude → Grok → rule-based fallback. If both APIs are down or the daily budget is exhausted, the bot falls back to a conservative rule-based decision (raises confidence threshold, halves size). The bot never blocks.

Honest limit

The LLM is judgment, not magic

Claude doesn't predict the future — it sanity-checks signals against context. Veto rate is typically 30–50% on Free/Starter (cheaper Haiku model), 15–25% on Pro/Elite (Sonnet/Opus). Lower veto rate ≠ better; sometimes the AI should be skeptical.

03 / Risk gates

Six gates between a signal and an order

Even after AI ratification, every order passes through six independent risk checks. Any one of them can block the trade.

  • Bayesian Kelly sizer · Posterior shrinks toward zero with few samples or wide variance. No fixed bet sizes.
  • Volatility targeting · EWMA realized-vol forecast → leverage. Caps at 2.0×, floors at 0.25×.
  • HMM regime gate · 3-state Hidden Markov Model. Crisis regime → 30% sizing or out entirely.
  • VPIN toxicity halt · Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading. Above the 95th percentile, entries halt — informed flow detected.
  • Cross-strategy correlation monitor · If two strategies' returns correlate > 0.85, the bot warns and underweights the redundant one.
  • Hard max-drawdown circuit breaker · Drawdown breaches your limit (default 15%) → all entries halt, existing positions trail-stop out.
Risk warning

You can lose money

These gates reduce risk; they don't eliminate it. A flash crash, gap-down on news, or a bug can still cause losses. Never trade money you can't afford to lose. Paper-trade until you trust the bot's behaviour. Risk-modal acknowledgment is required on first dashboard visit.

04 / Order execution

Bracket orders + smart routing

When all checks pass, the bot submits a bracket order to Alpaca (or a force-entry to Freqtrade for crypto). Bracket = entry + stop-loss + take-profit as a single atomic unit, so you can't end up with a naked position when the API hiccups.

Order types

  • Smart (default) · Limit at price ± slippage tolerance. Falls back to market if the limit doesn't fill within retry window.
  • TWAP · Time-Weighted slice for large orders. Default 5 slices over 30s.
  • VWAP · Volume-Weighted participation, target 10% of bar volume per slice.

Per-cycle entry cap is 3 trades (configurable) — prevents simultaneous signals from draining buying power. A virtual capital cap (default $5,000) caps total deployed exposure regardless of strategy preference.

05 / Self-learning

The bot labels its own mistakes

After every closed trade, a labeller compares the realized P&L against the trade's MFE (max favorable excursion) and MAE (max adverse excursion) to classify the outcome:

LabelMeaningTuner response
goodRealized ≈ MFE; clean execution.No change.
sold_too_earlyExited < 50% of MFE.Widen target_atr_mult, loosen trail.
held_too_longHit peak, gave back > 60%.Tighten trail, lower target.
cut_loss_lateMAE > 1.2× stop distance.Tighten stop_atr_mult.
false_signal< 10 min hold + loss.Raise confidence threshold.
choppySmall P&L either way.Be more selective.

The tuner uses EMA smoothing (α = 0.15) so single bad trades don't whipsaw parameters. A contextual multi-armed bandit tracks the win-rate of each (strategy, signal_combo, regime) tuple — Thompson sampling balances exploration and exploitation. Every change is logged; you can see exactly which parameter changed when in your dashboard.

06 / How we make money

Flat subscription. No revenue share. Ever.

We charge $0–$199/mo. That's it. No commissions, no spread markup, no profit-share, no opaque execution fees, no kickbacks from brokers.

Why it matters: the most common predatory model in this space is "free + 30% of profits." On a $1K account at a realistic 1.5%/mo, that's $4.50 to them and $10.50 to you — but they can encourage churn-y strategies that generate fees regardless of net outcome. About 60% of retail algo-traders are profitable annually, vs. 5–10% of manual day-traders. So the bot does help — but it doesn't print money.

Things that can wipe you out

  • Overnight gaps · Stops don't work when the market is closed. A 30% gap-down can blow through your stop.
  • Black-swan correlated drawdowns · 2008, 2020-03, 2022. Risk parity helps but doesn't protect against everything-goes-down.
  • API outages · Alpaca, your broker, our cloud — all have uptime SLAs < 100%. The bot can miss exits during downtime.
  • Model drift · Strategies trained on 2023 data won't perform identically in 2027. The self-learning loop helps but doesn't fix regime shifts.
  • You · Manually overriding the bot during a drawdown is the most common cause of permanent loss.

09 / FAQ

Common questions

Can I see the source code?

The bot strategies and learning engine are documented in your dashboard's "Brain" page. The proprietary tuning, prompts, and feature flags are not open-source — but every decision is auditable from your account.

What broker do I need?

Alpaca for stocks (free signup, paper-trading by default). For crypto, you can either use Alpaca's crypto pairs or run your own Freqtrade instance and point Ferchaud at it.

Where is my money?

In your broker account. Ferchaud never holds your funds. We have read+trade permissions via API keys you control — you can revoke at any time.

What about taxes?

Your broker provides 1099-B / equivalent tax documents. Ferchaud does not generate tax forms. Wash-sale rules, holding-period rules, and short-term capital gains all apply normally — automated trading does not exempt you from any of them.

Can I customize the strategies?

Pro and Elite tiers can adjust strategy weights and individual parameter floors/ceilings. Custom strategies (your code, our infra) are an Elite-only service — email elite@ferchaud.com.

What if I want to stop?

Click "Stop bot" on the dashboard. Cancel from billing portal. Revoke broker API keys. Done — no clawback, no exit fee.
