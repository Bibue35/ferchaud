5. Automated Trading — Specific Risks

Automated trading carries unique risks that differ from manual trading.
  • System risk: Software bugs, API failures, and network outages can cause missed trades, duplicate trades, or unintended positions.
  • Over-optimization: Strategies optimized on historical data may perform poorly in live markets (overfitting).
  • Market regime change: A strategy can fail suddenly when market conditions shift, with no advance warning.
  • Execution risk: Automated orders may execute at prices worse than backtested prices due to slippage and liquidity constraints.
  • Concentration risk: Running multiple correlated strategies simultaneously can increase, not decrease, risk.
  • Runaway trading: A malfunctioning bot can generate excessive trades, losses, or margin calls before being stopped.

You are solely responsible for monitoring your automated trading account, setting appropriate risk limits, and stopping the bot when necessary. Ferchaud provides tools to set stop-losses and daily loss limits, and you are strongly encouraged to use them.
