The loop

Scan, ratify, learn — every 10 seconds.

Three stages, repeating endlessly. Each stage is auditable: you can read the rationale of every decision, the parameters the bot adapted, and the mistake it labelled itself with after each exit.

01 / Scan

Scan 12,000+ symbols

Full-market scanner finds movers. 13 strategies (mean-reversion, momentum, stat-arb, multi-factor ML) generate candidate trades. Grok pulls live X/Twitter context.

02 / Ratify

Claude reads every trade

Each candidate goes to Claude (or Grok) with regime, recent winrate, mistake history, portfolio context. The AI vetoes, sizes, and writes the rationale in one JSON object.

03 / Learn

Adapts after every close

Each closed trade is labelled — sold-too-early, late-stop, false-signal, clean. The tuner widens stops or raises confidence thresholds when the same mistake repeats.

Built on what works

Battle-tested infra. AI on top.

Alpaca runs the brokerage side. Freqtrade handles crypto. Both have years of real-world hardening. We don't reinvent execution — we make it think with a Claude/Grok decision layer and a learning engine that watches every fill.

Bayesian Kelly sizing

Position sizes shrink toward zero when edge is uncertain. No fixed-fraction bets.

HMM regime detector

Three-state Hidden Markov Model. Bull = full risk. Crisis = 30% sizing or out.

VPIN toxicity halt

Volume-Synchronized Probability of Informed Trading detects toxic flow and halts entries when smart money is moving.

Hard max-drawdown circuit breaker

If portfolio drawdown breaches your limit, every strategy halts immediately. No exceptions.
