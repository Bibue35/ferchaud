HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to smarter trades

Connect your broker, choose a strategy, and let the algorithm handle entries and exits with precision timing.

01

Connect Your Broker

Link your Alpaca or Robinhood account with API keys. Your credentials are encrypted and never stored in plain text. Takes under 2 minutes.

02

Select a Strategy

Choose from momentum, mean-reversion, or breakout strategies. Each one is backtested on historical data so you can review past performance.

03

Deploy and Monitor

Activate live or paper trading. The algorithm scans for signals, executes trades, and sends you notifications. You stay in control at all times.

FEATURES

Built for serious traders

Real-Time Signals
