FAQ

Common questions

Do I need trading experience to use Ferchaud?
No. Start with paper trading and explore how strategies work before committing real capital. The dashboard explains every signal in plain English.
Is my money safe? Who holds it?
Ferchaud never holds your money. All capital stays in your own brokerage account (Alpaca, IBKR, etc.). We only send trade signals — you remain in full control.
What is paper trading?
Paper trading simulates real trades without real money. It's included in every plan so you can test strategies risk-free before going live.
How does the Alpha Combination Engine work?
It uses the Fundamental Law of Active Management: IR = IC × √N. Every strategy produces a signal score. The engine weights them by their independent information contribution (not just raw performance) and combines them into one optimal mega-signal.
Which brokers are supported?
Alpaca (stocks, ETFs, options — free), Interactive Brokers (futures, full multi-asset), and OANDA (FX). More integrations coming soon.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard in one click. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Your strategies continue running until the end of your billing period.
