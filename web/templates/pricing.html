Frequently asked questions

What does "revenue share" mean? ▼
When the bot makes a profitable trade, we take a small percentage of the gains. For example, on the Pro plan: if the bot earns $1,000, you keep $900 and we keep $100. On losing trades, we take nothing.
Is my money safe? ▼
Your funds are held at Alpaca Markets (FINRA-member broker). We never have direct custody of your money. The bot trades on your behalf via API.
Can I cancel anytime? ▼
Yes. Cancel anytime from your dashboard billing settings. No cancellation fees. You keep your remaining balance.
What stocks does the bot trade? ▼
The bot scans 12,000+ US equities and targets stocks priced $0.50-$50 with strong momentum and volume signals. It avoids mega-caps and focuses on explosive small/mid-cap movers.
